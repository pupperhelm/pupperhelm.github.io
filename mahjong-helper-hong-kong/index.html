About the app

Mahjong Helper is a calm table-side companion for Hong Kong-style / Cantonese Mahjong. Learn the tiles, Chow, Pung, Kong, calling and winning; enter a physical hand by tapping tile pictures; validate its structure; and see why it scores under Hong Kong Old Style — Standard Beginner Preset. English explanations include Traditional Chinese Mahjong terms. The app supports concealed and declared melds, Flowers and Seasons, configurable minimum faan and caps, special hands, Quick Help, searchable lessons, and friendly practice questions. It works offline after installation with no account, ads, analytics, camera, AI or cloud service. Rules and faan values vary by family and club, so agree on your table's house rules before play.

FAQ

Using Mahjong Helper

Is this a digital Mahjong game?

No. Mahjong Helper is a table-side reference, hand checker and scoring companion for physical tiles.

Does it use one universal Hong Kong ruleset?

No. It starts with a clearly labelled Hong Kong Old Style beginner preset and exposes common house variations.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Lessons, tile artwork, hand validation and scoring are bundled in the app.
