About the app

New to Hong Kong Mahjong? Sitting at a table with experienced players? Mahjong Helper is a calm, offline companion for learning the tiles, scoring a hand, and tracking a live game with physical tiles.

Built for real tables, it combines a Hong Kong Mahjong score tracker, hand checker, rules guide, and beginner lessons in one focused app.

Track a live game

  • Add four player names and starting seats.
  • Follow round wind, seat winds, dealer, and cumulative scores.
  • Record discard wins, self-draws, or draws in a quick popup.
  • Use Standard payouts or save named tables for your family or club.
  • Review every hand, undo mistakes, and resume the game anytime.

Learn, check, and score

Identify tiles, learn calls, enter a physical hand, validate its structure, and see why each scoring element earns faan under a visible Hong Kong Old Style preset.

Made for the whole table

Large controls, screen-reader tile descriptions, dark mode, Android font scaling, English explanations, and Traditional Chinese terminology are included. The app works offline with no account, ads, analytics, camera, or cloud service.

Mahjong rules, faan values, and payouts vary between families, clubs, and regions. Agree on the table rules before play. Mahjong Helper keeps the selected rules and payout table visible and configurable.

Mahjong Helper is for Hong Kong-style / Cantonese Mahjong. It supports a physical game; it does not simulate opponents or replace agreement among the players at the table.

FAQ

Using Mahjong Helper

Can it track a live Mahjong game?

Yes. Add four players, follow round and seat winds, record discard wins, self-draws or draws, and keep a persistent score history.

Can I use my family’s payout table?

Yes. Start with the Standard table, edit its values, and save separate named payout tables for reuse.

Is this a digital Mahjong game?

No. Mahjong Helper is a table-side reference, hand checker and live score tracker for physical tiles.

Does it use one universal Hong Kong ruleset?

No. It starts with a clearly labelled Hong Kong Old Style beginner preset and exposes common house variations.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Live game tracking, saved payout tables, lessons, tile artwork, hand validation and scoring stay on the device.
