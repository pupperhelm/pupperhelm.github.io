Mahjong Helper is an offline learning and scoring companion for Hong Kong-style Mahjong. The app does not require an account and does not provide cloud synchronization.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your selected house rules, entered hand, practice progress, and display preferences locally on your Android device. This information is not sent to the publisher’s servers. You can remove it by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

Advertising

Mahjong Helper contains no advertising SDK, advertising identifiers, analytics SDK, account system, or cloud sync. The publisher does not sell or share your locally entered hand data.

Sharing and retention

The publisher does not operate an account database for Mahjong Helper. The publisher does not receive a copy of your locally entered hand from the app.

Children

Mahjong Helper is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice when the app or its data practices change. For privacy questions, contact pupperhelm@gmail.com.
