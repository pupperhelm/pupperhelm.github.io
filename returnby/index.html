The idea

A calmer way to keep track of what you bought.

When a return window is closing or a warranty question comes up, the important details are often scattered across emails, photos, downloads, and paper. ReturnBy gives each purchase a place for its receipt and the dates worth checking.

How it works

From receipt to next step.

You stay in control of the details before they become a saved purchase.

01

Bring in your proof

Import a photo, screenshot, PDF, email file, or shared text. You can also scan a paper receipt or enter a purchase manually.

02

Check the details

ReturnBy reads useful details on your device and prepares dates and retailer guidance. Review and edit anything uncertain before saving.

03

Find it later

Search your purchases, open the saved proof, and see upcoming return, warranty, or product dates in one timeline.

Made for the follow-through

Privacy, plainly

Your receipts belong to you.

ReturnBy does not require an account or automatically read your inbox. Receipt reading happens on your device. When you request return-policy research, the app may visit public retailer pages and search using the merchant name and purchase country. Your receipt files and purchase details are not sent with those lookups.

Good to know

A few useful answers.

Are return dates guaranteed?

No. Receipt reading and retailer terms can be incomplete or out of date. Check the policy and your purchase circumstances before relying on a date.

Does it connect to my email?

No. You choose which files or text to import or share with ReturnBy. There is no automatic inbox access.

Can I move my records to another phone?

You can export and restore a manual ZIP backup. It includes purchase records and originals and is not password-protected, so store it somewhere you trust.

Is it available now?

ReturnBy is an Android app in development. For availability or support questions, email Pupperhelm Studio.
